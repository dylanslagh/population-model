\section{Probabilistic baseline}

\wip{A genuine Finland fixture has been extracted through the official
University of Washington accessors and reconciled. This section will be
completed only after bulk extraction and conversion into age-specific
schedules.}

The baseline will begin from 1,000 annual UW trajectories of total fertility
and paired female and male life expectancy aligned to WPP 2024. These are UW
products, not official UN posterior draws. Their native accessors can apply
stored alignment shifts, so selected binary files cannot be read in isolation
without risking a silent change in values \citep{uw2024downloads}.

The source boundary preserves total fertility and life expectancy at full
precision together with their original trajectory identities. A separately
versioned schedule converter then expands total fertility into age-specific
fertility and life expectancy into age- and sex-specific survival. Conversion
is accepted only if the detailed schedules reconstruct the compact source
quantities within stated tolerances.

Female and male life expectancy trajectories are paired by UW's joint model.
The fertility and mortality archives are separate products. If equal ordinal
pairing is used across those products, it will be a reproducible project
assumption and will not be described as a joint fertility-mortality posterior.

The UW archives contain 236 locations while WPP 2024 contains 237. The extracted
code sets confirm that the omitted location is Holy See (M49 336); no posterior
trajectory is invented to fill it. The source also contains a 2023 anchor; the
project preserves that value for validation and begins projected rate draws in
2024.

These source trajectories end in 2100. Any projection from 2101 through 2150
therefore requires separately named extension policies for fertility,
mortality, and migration. The present download covers fertility and mortality;
the resulting ensemble will be conditional on an explicit migration
assumption, base population, and sex ratio at birth rather than being described
as a complete probabilistic WPP population projection.
