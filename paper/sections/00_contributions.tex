\section*{Author contributions and responsibility}
\label{sec:contributions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Author contributions and responsibility}

Two contributors named in the byline are language models. They are identified to
disclose their substantive roles and to make the production of the analysis and
manuscript auditable.

\textbf{Dylan Slagh} conceived the project, set its scope and its constraints,
made every decision about what the model would and would not attempt, chose the
questions the paper answers and the order it answers them in, and reviewed all
of it. The standing rules the work is held to --- in particular that no
mechanism parameter may be fitted to the series it explains, and that a stored
prediction may never be overwritten --- are enforced throughout at his
insistence.

\textbf{Claude Opus 5} implemented the mechanism layer, the migration and rate
continuations, the uncertainty analyses and the reproducibility machinery, and
drafted the manuscript.

\textbf{ChatGPT 5.6 Sol} reviewed the scientific argument and mathematical
claims, clarified the distinction between terminal-rate and accumulated-population
thresholds (\Cref{sec:boundary}), examined the interpretation of the constructed
covariance (\Cref{sec:response}), and provided comprehensive editing of the
manuscript and supplementary material.

Current journal authorship policies generally do not admit non-human authors.
The byline therefore discloses the division of labour rather than asserting that
the language models satisfy those policies. Dylan Slagh takes responsibility for
the analysis and manuscript.
