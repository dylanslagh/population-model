\section{Conclusion}

Long-run population projection is most useful when it can be wrong for a stated
reason. This project fixes and validates a demographic accounting layer,
reports a preliminary historical backtest, and establishes an immutable
prediction format. The probabilistic baseline and mechanism comparison remain
under construction. The completed paper will be successful not if it identifies
a dramatic population total for 2150, but if it makes competing mechanisms
comparable and commits them to nearer-term outcomes that can change our
confidence in those mechanisms.
